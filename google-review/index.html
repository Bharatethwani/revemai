Frequently Asked Questions

Will Google Remove AI-Generated Reviews?

No — not simply because AI was used to help write them.

Google's policies focus on the authenticity of the review, not whether AI assisted in writing it. RevmeAI does not automatically post reviews or submit anything to Google. Instead, it acts as a writing assistant to help customers express their genuine experiences more clearly and naturally.

How it works securely:
  • The customer scans your Google Review QR Code.
  • RevmeAI suggests a review based on their experience.
  • The customer manually edits and chooses to copy/paste the review into Google.

Google only removes reviews that are fabricated, from non-visitors, manipulative, or spam content. As long as your customers reflect their real experience, using AI to draft the wording is entirely compliant.

How does the Free Trial work?

When you select a paid plan, you are granted a completely free period (e.g., 15, 30, or 60 days depending on the plan). During this time, you have full access to all features to test out the platform before your official billing period begins.

Do I need to buy a domain for the free website?

No! RevmeAI provides you with a free, fully-hosted AI-generated business website out of the box. You do not need to purchase a domain name or pay for separate web hosting.

Do my customers need to download an app?

Not at all. Your customers simply open their smartphone camera, scan the QR code you display, and the RevmeAI web app opens instantly in their default browser.
